## 1. Safety Philosophy: Non-Destructive Alerts

The Grade-Lesion Mismatch Detection Engine is designed as a **strictly non-destructive clinical safety alert system**. 

### Core Safeguard Principles
1. **Zero Silent Grade Overrides:** Under no circumstances does the engine silently alter, downgrade, or overwrite the predicted DR grade generated by the neural network classifier.
2. **Transparent Divergence Flagging:** When global classifier attention diverges from physical lesion segmentation, the discrepancy is highlighted as a high-visibility clinical alert.
3. **Preservation of Provenance:** The original model predictions, raw probability distribution, calibrated confidence, and U-Net segmentations are permanently preserved in the patient's medical record for regulatory traceability.

## 2. Explicit Mismatch Rules (`MISMATCH_R01` – `MISMATCH_R06`)

### `MISMATCH_R01`: High DR Grade Without Segmented Lesions
- **Severity:** `CRITICAL` (for Grade $\ge 3$) / `WARNING` (for Grade 2)
- **Category:** `FALSE_POSITIVE_RISK`
- **Condition:** Predicted Grade $\ge 2$ (Moderate, Severe, or Proliferative DR), but U-Net segmentation finds 0 microaneurysms, hemorrhages, or exudates.
- **Clinical Implication:** High risk of false positive driven by optical glare, retinal pigment epithelium (RPE) variation, or background camera artifacts.
- **Recommended Action:** Ophthalmologist must visually examine the high-resolution fundus to confirm microvascular integrity before scheduling referral.
- **Non-Destructive Notice:** Predicted grade is preserved; screening case is flagged as `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED`.

---

### `MISMATCH_R02`: Grade 0 Predicted With Substantial Lesion Evidence
- **Severity:** `CRITICAL`
- **Category:** `FALSE_NEGATIVE_RISK`
- **Condition:** Predicted Grade $= 0$ (No DR), but U-Net detects hemorrhages ($N \ge 2$), hard exudates ($N \ge 2$), or microaneurysms ($N \ge 5$).
- **Clinical Implication:** Severe danger of under-diagnosis / false reassurance. Intraretinal hemorrhages and microaneurysms indicate active retinopathy that requires monitoring or intervention.
- **Recommended Action:** Mandatory urgent ophthalmologist review to prevent untreated diabetic retinopathy progression.
- **Non-Destructive Notice:** Grade 0 is retained in the audit log; referral priority is escalated to `CRITICAL`.

---

### `MISMATCH_R03`: Mild NPDR With Moderate/Severe Lesion Features
- **Severity:** `WARNING`
- **Category:** `UNDER_GRADING_RISK`
- **Condition:** Predicted Grade $= 1$ (Mild NPDR), but segmentation identifies hemorrhages ($N \ge 5$) or hard exudates ($N \ge 5$).
- **Clinical Implication:** By ICDR definition, Mild NPDR presents microaneurysms only. Multiple hemorrhages or exudates indicate Moderate or Severe NPDR.
- **Recommended Action:** Evaluate for clinical upgrade to Moderate NPDR (Grade 2) and schedule macular OCT if exudates encroach on the fovea.
- **Non-Destructive Notice:** Grade 1 is retained for clinical review.

---

### `MISMATCH_R04`: Proliferative DR Predicted With Sparse Lesions
- **Severity:** `CRITICAL`
- **Category:** `DISCREPANCY_ALERT`
- **Condition:** Predicted Grade $= 4$ (Proliferative DR), but total segmented lesions $\le 2$.
- **Clinical Implication:** Proliferative DR typically exhibits extensive retinopathy signs, vitreous haze, or prominent neovascularization. Sparse lesions suggest potential artifact confusion.
- **Recommended Action:** Urgent specialist evaluation (slit-lamp biomicroscopy and fluorescein angiography if indicated) to confirm or rule out active neovascular fronds.
- **Non-Destructive Notice:** High clinical urgency maintained without tampering with raw classifier output.

---

### `MISMATCH_R05`: High Lesion Surface Area In Low-Grade Prediction
- **Severity:** `CRITICAL`
- **Category:** `EXTENSIVE_PATHOLOGY_DISCREPANCY`
- **Condition:** Predicted Grade $\le 1$, but total lesion surface coverage exceeds $2.0\%$ of the retinal field.
- **Clinical Implication:** Heavy pathological density directly contradicts early or benign DR classification.
- **Recommended Action:** Immediate ophthalmologist re-evaluation across all four retinal quadrants.
- **Non-Destructive Notice:** Flagged as high-priority human review.

---

### `MISMATCH_R06`: Explainability–Segmentation Spatial Divergence
- **Severity:** `INFO`
- **Category:** `ATTRIBUTION_DISSONANCE`
- **Condition:** Grad-CAM peak activation score $< 0.20$ over verified lesion clusters ($N \ge 5$).
- **Clinical Implication:** Classifier attention is directed away from identifiable microvascular lesions, focusing instead on peripheral or non-lesion landmarks.
- **Recommended Action:** Clinician should use the 3-panel synchronized explainability workspace to correlate attention heatmaps with segmented clusters.
- **Non-Destructive Notice:** Informational notice to support clinical interpretation.
